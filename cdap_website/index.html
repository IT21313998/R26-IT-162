Executive Summary

Project Overview & Abstract

Addressing recurrent flood disasters in Sri Lanka's Kelani River Basin through localized technology.

Abstract

Flooding is a recurrent and devastating natural hazard in Sri Lanka. The catastrophic Cyclone Ditwah in November 2025 affected approximately 2.2 million people across all 25 districts, resulting in 646 fatalities and displacing more than 165,000 residents. Despite advancements in meteorological forecasting, a critical gap remains in providing localized and actionable flood warnings to riverine communities.

Current early warning systems rely primarily on manual river gauge readings and centralized bulletins distributed through television or SMS. These approaches suffer from low spatial resolution, delayed information dissemination, and limited public engagement. This research proposes an intelligent IoT-based flood early warning system that integrates hyperlocal rainfall monitoring with machine learning-based multi-horizon forecasting (Bi-LSTM, LightGBM, and XGBoost) and a Distance-Based Dynamic Flood Risk Scoring (DFRS) model.

Hyperlocal IoT Sensing

Solar-powered ESP32 nodes equipped with tipping bucket & ultrasonic rain gauges and SIM800L GPRS / LoRa telemetry to deliver minute-by-minute rainfall data.

Multi-Horizon ML Forecasting

Gradient Boosting and Bi-LSTM models fused with atmospheric telemetry to accurately predict 24h, 48h, and 72h rainfall depths with >85% accuracy.

Early Warning & DFRS

Distance-Based Dynamic Flood Risk Scoring calculating personalized flood exposure and transmitting trilingual warnings (Sinhala, Tamil, English).

System Design

End-to-End Architectural Flow

Bridging field hardware, cloud message brokers, AI predictive models, and downstream flood warning consumers.

1. Field IoT Sensing

ESP32 + Ultrasonic Rain Cup + GSM SIM800L sends real-time rainfall to EMQX MQTT broker.

2. Atmospheric Fusion

Open-Meteo live API pulls Kelani Basin barometric pressure, dew point, humidity, and wind vectors.

3. ML Inference Engine

Trained LightGBM & Bi-LSTM predict rainfall depth (mm) for Day +1, Day +2, and Day +3 horizons.

4. Partner DFRS & Alert

FastAPI endpoint provides real-time inputs to Student B's Water Level & Flood Risk model.
